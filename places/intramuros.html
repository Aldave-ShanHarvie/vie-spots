Intramuros

About Intramuros

Intramuros is a historic walled area located in Manila. It is known for its Spanish-era architecture, old buildings, churches, and historic landmarks. It is a great place to visit for students and tourists who want to learn more about the history of Manila while spending time with friends.

I recommend Intramuros because it is accessible from TUP and offers many places to explore. Visitors can walk around the historic streets, see old structures, take photos, and find places to eat nearby. It can also be a good place to visit during free time or after school.
