About Me

Shan Harvie Aldave

I’m a resourceful and budget-conscious student who has discovered many great spots without spending too much.

I created Vie-Spots because I wanted to share places where students can eat, relax, and spend time with friends without going too far from TUP. These recommendations are based on places I have personally visited and experienced.

My recommendations are worth trusting because they are based on my own experiences and are focused on places that are accessible and practical for students. I also consider factors such as location, affordability, and whether the place is enjoyable to visit with friends.
